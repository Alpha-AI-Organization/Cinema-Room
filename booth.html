Cinema sound booth

This small window gives the film real 3D sound and makes the hall light follow the real picture. Keep it open while you watch.

  1. Press Connect.
  2. In the browser box, pick the tab named Cinema Room.
  3. Turn on Also share tab audio, then press Share.
